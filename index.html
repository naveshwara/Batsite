Where light
becomes memory.

Ten years behind the lens. Long enough to stop chasing pretty and start chasing true — the frame that makes a stranger feel something they can't quite name, then carry it around for years without knowing why.

"A film should make you feel something. A campaign should make you do something. I build both — in the same edit suite."

Camera, timeline, and the grade are all one room to me. I shoot it, I cut it, I color it — and when something arrives broken, I quietly fix it in the grade and nobody ever knows. The work is the fingerprint; this is just where I keep it.
